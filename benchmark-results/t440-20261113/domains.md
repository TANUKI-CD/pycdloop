24 blocks; each cell is the mean over blocks of nanoseconds per product with a two-sided 95% Student-t interval. Timing is in Python with the garbage collector disabled, after 2 untimed passes, for at least 20 ms per observation, and includes the call overhead of the package. A ratio is the geometric mean of per-block ratios with its 95% interval.

All 5920 verified products agree with an independent evaluation of the definition.

`split` multiplies through the decomposition of the loop algebra and `table` evaluates the definition. `N` is the dimension of the loop algebra of `Q_n`, which is `2^(n+1)`. Finite-field products are verified exactly against schoolbook field arithmetic; complex products are verified with a norm-scaled tolerance.

Over the fields of characteristic 2 the algebra has no split form, and the `split` columns give the `lift` algorithm: the split algorithm applied to the coefficients read as integers over a large prime field, followed by reduction.
